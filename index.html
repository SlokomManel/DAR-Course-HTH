With poor ratings and negative reviews, occupancy is LOW. Guests prefer other hotels.
📐 How We Diagnose:
We infer Cleanliness (root cause) based on guest feedback:
Cleanliness = (0.40 × Rating) + (0.60 × Reviews)
Then we calculate Occupancy (the business impact):
Occupancy % = (0.4 × Rating) + (0.3 × Reviews) + (0.3 × Cleanliness)
💡 Key Learning:
✓ This is ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS. We don't know directly WHY ratings are low, but we can infer it's likely cleanliness.
✓ Multiple factors matter. Occupancy depends on ratings, reviews, AND cleanliness together—not just one.
✓ Diagnosis leads to action. If cleanliness is low, the hotel manager would invest in housekeeping to fix it.
✓ This is how businesses work. You see a problem (low ratings), diagnose the cause (probably cleanliness), then take action.
