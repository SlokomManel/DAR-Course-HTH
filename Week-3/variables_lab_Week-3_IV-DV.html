Two causes, one effect

Scenario: A hotel manager wants to measure the effect of room cleanliness and staff friendliness on the guest rating. Move the two causes (independent variables, IV) and see how the guest rating (dependent variable, DV) changes.

Note: the effects in this lab are simulated, we built them in. A real manager cannot see them directly. How would a real manager find out? That is what surveys are for.

Level of measurement: ordinal (1 to 5 stars)

Try it: move one cause at a time. Which arrow is thicker? “Big”, “medium” and “small” are simple labels for this lab. In real research you must test whether a cause really has an effect.
